# Sample Itineraries

These are real outputs from the current system, generated on 2026-09-28 from two sets of intake
answers. Nothing was edited by hand: the destinations, the "feeling behind your journey" text and the
day-by-day plans are exactly what the API returned. The full API responses are in `docs/samples/`
(the Google API key has been removed from the photo links).

**How to read them**

- **The feeling headline** always comes from the feeling the traveler selected, never from the destination.
  The sentences under it were written for this specific itinerary and checked automatically:
  - 2–3 sentences;
  - no promises about how the traveler will feel;
  - they refer to at least two experiences that really are in the plan.
- **If a plan can't support the chosen feeling,** the system re-plans once. If it still can't, it flags the
  mismatch instead of showing the text.
- **Prices are estimates.** No stay, ticket or activity has been booked or checked for availability. Places
  marked "Yes" were matched to a real business on Google Maps. "Not verified" means the activity is
  a suggestion without a confirmed venue.
- **Meal stops are skipped when an activity already covers that time** (for example, a full day out on the water).
- **Results vary between runs** because the itinerary is written by an AI model. The rules and checks stay the same.
