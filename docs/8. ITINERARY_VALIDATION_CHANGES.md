# API Changes: Itinerary Validation and Guest-Facing Output

**For:** the backend team that calls this AI service and the frontend team that renders its results.
**Effective:** 2026-10-05. The request bodies are unchanged; the itinerary response adds fields and changes how some
existing fields should be displayed.

---

## 1. Summary

| What | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Geography | One hotel; activities could be hours apart | **One base per stop** (the stay). Every experience and restaurant is within **60 minutes** of the base and of the previous stop, measured from coordinates with Google Routes. Too-far items are replaced with a closer place or removed. |
| Distant regions | Mixed into the same days | **Multi-stop itinerary** (trips of 4+ nights only): a stay per region, a transfer with buffers (and a ferry note), and a lighter travel day |
| Feeling copy | "THE FEELING: …", "You chose …", "Why it fits …" | **"Designed to help you feel: _[primary feeling]_"** plus 1–2 sentences about this itinerary |
| Recommendation reasons | Generic ("a convenient stop near the day's planned route") | **`why_selected`** on every stay, experience and restaurant, specific to the guest and the place |
| Raw values | `PRICE_LEVEL_MODERATE`, "(approximately)", source-review text | Guest labels such as **"$$ · Moderate"**; internal review status moved out of guest fields |
| Total price | `total_cost_estimate` (hotel + per-person costs × party, labelled as an activity total) | **`price_breakdown`**: accommodation, experiences, dining, transport, taxes; the total is the exact sum and is **withheld** when a price is missing |
| Final check | None | **`validation`** checklist before display and save |
| "Ready to book" | Not modelled | **`booking_status`**: only ready when every component is verified (today: never, until booking APIs are connected) |
| Saved sessions | In memory only; lost on restart | **Stored durably**; saved searches reopen after a restart |
| CORS | Not configured | **Configured** via `CORS_ALLOW_ORIGINS` |

## 2. What the frontend should show (and never show)

**Show to guests:**

| Field | How |
|---|---|
| `feeling_block.markdown` | As is: `Designed to help you feel: **Restored**` then the explanation. Or build it from `feeling_block.primary_feeling` (bold) + `feeling_block.explanation`. Remove the old "You chose" / "Why it fits" sections. |
| `booking_status.guest_label` | Once, near the price. Never show a "Book now"/"Ready to book" state unless `booking_status.ready_to_book` is `true`. |
| `stops[]` | One section per stop: `base_area`, nights, `stay` card. |
| `stops[].stay` | `name`, `address`, `rating`, `price_level` (already a guest label, may be empty), `average_nightly_price`, `why_selected`, `photos`. |
| `tour_plan[].activities[]` | `activity_name`, `activity_time`, `activity_description`, **`why_selected`**, `activity_address`, `activity_image`, travel line from `travel_minutes_from_previous` + `travel_from` (e.g. "About 12 min from your hotel"). Restaurants: `price_indication`, `rating`. |
| `price_breakdown` | One row per `lines[]` entry (`label`, `amount`, `basis`), then `total_label` + `total`. If `total` is `null`, show `total_withheld_reason` instead of a total. Show `applies_to` and `what_may_vary` once. |
| `guest_notes[]` | Once, in one "Good to know" area. Each note is already specific and deduplicated. |

**Never render to guests:** `validation`, `adjustments` (their guest-safe text is already in `guest_notes`),
`booking_status.reasons`, `budget_check`, `travel_time_source`, `place_id`, `business_status`, coordinates,
and on suggestion cards: `verification` (including `verification.internal_notes`), `evidence`, `score_breakdown`,
`restriction_checks`, `distance_check`.

**Item types** (`tour_plan[].activities[].item_type`):

| `item_type` | Meaning | Display |
|---|---|---|
| `experience` | A planned experience | Normal card |
| `meal` | A restaurant, or `open_slot: true` when we deliberately left the meal flexible | Restaurant card; open slots as a light line |
| `transfer` | Move between stops (`transfer_minutes`, `transfer_buffer_minutes`, `includes_ferry`) | A travel card at the start of the day |
| `free_time` | Intentionally unscheduled time | A light "Open time" line |

`tour_plan[].day_type` is `"transfer"` on the first day at a new stop.

**`validation.status`:** `passed`, `passed_with_warnings`, or `failed`. When `display_ready` is `false` (an error such
as no stay found or a leg over the limit), do not show the itinerary as a finished plan. Offer to regenerate it
instead. Issue codes: `STOP_BASE_UNLOCATED`, `STAY_NOT_FOUND`, `STAY_NOT_CENTRAL`, `TRANSFER_MISSING`,
`LEG_OVER_LIMIT`, `NO_DRIVABLE_ROUTE`, `INTERNAL_WORDING` (errors); `ITEM_UNLOCATED`, `REASON_MISSING`,
`TRAVEL_TIME_ESTIMATED`, `PRICING_INCOMPLETE` (warnings).

**`total_cost_estimate`** now equals `price_breakdown.total` and may be `null`.

## 4. Destination suggestions (`POST /get_suggested_city`)

- New `primary_feeling` on each suggestion. Render `Designed to help you feel: **{primary_feeling}**` and then
  `description`.
- `description`, `match_reasons`, `tradeoffs` and `unresolved_facts` are rewritten in guest language: no
  "our editors tag", "catalog", pipe-separated tags or "Source review pending".
- Internal review notes moved to `verification.internal_notes` (do not display).
- `warnings` is still identical to `tradeoffs` for older clients. **Render only one of them** (prefer `tradeoffs`),
  otherwise every note appears twice.

## 5. Search History (saved searches and the eye icon)

Changed in this service:

- **Sessions are stored durably** (SQLite, `SESSION_DB_PATH`, default `data/sessions.sqlite3`). Before, every
  restart or deploy erased them, so a saved search pointed at a `session_id` / `activity_session_id` that returned
  `404`. `GET /session/{id}` and `GET /activity_session/{id}` now work after restarts.
- **CORS**: set `CORS_ALLOW_ORIGINS` to the frontend origins (comma-separated) to allow cross-origin calls with
  credentials. When unset, any origin may call without credentials.

Still to check in the main backend and frontend (outside this repository):

1. The signed-in user's token or cookie is sent on the Search History request (same handling as the login pages).
2. The history endpoint returns only that user's records.
3. The page shows a loading state, not a brief "no results", while history loads.
4. A clear message with a retry button when loading fails, plus server-side logging of failed history requests.
5. The eye icon opens the stored `activity_session_id` with `GET /activity_session/{id}`.

## 6. Configuration

| Setting | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `GOOGLE_API_KEY` | Must have **Places API (New)** and **Routes API** enabled. Routes is now used for the route matrix (day legs) and for transfers between stops. |
| `CORS_ALLOW_ORIGINS` | Frontend origins allowed to call this API with credentials |
| `SESSION_DB_PATH` | Session storage file; empty string keeps sessions in memory only |
| `VIATOR_API_KEY`, `VIATOR_PARTNER_ID` | Viator Partner API (Full access). Optional: without a key no Viator call is made |
| `VIATOR_BASE_URL` | `https://api.viator.com/partner` (default) or the sandbox `https://api.sandbox.viator.com/partner` |

Expect more Google Routes usage: about one matrix request per itinerary day plus one route per transfer.

## 7. Travel date suggestions: `POST /recommend_travel_dates` (new)

Call it at step 10 when the guest picks **"My dates are flexible"** or **"I know the month or season"**, after
they enter the number of nights.

**Request:** the same fields as `/get_suggested_city` for steps 1–10, **without** `budget_per_night`, plus optional:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `earliest_check_in`, `latest_check_out` | The guest's range, if the frontend collects one. Without it: the selected month/season, or the next 6 months for flexible |
| `destination_id` | When the guest has already chosen a destination, its own climate is used |

`travel_timing: "exact_dates"` is rejected (`422`); so is a range shorter than `trip_nights`.

**How the dates are chosen (never random).** Every possible check-in in the window is scored on:

1. **Weather fit**: recorded monthly averages (highs, rainy days; Open-Meteo, last 5 years) for the destination,
   or the departure area on a nearby trip, against the guest's settings, moments and heat/cold restrictions.
   Without a known place, general seasonality for the guest's hemisphere, stated as such.
2. **Crowds**: public holidays in the departure country (including widely observed regional ones), Christmas–New
   Year, Easter and the summer school break. Restful or quiet trips avoid them; families with children are steered
   to school holidays.
3. **Day-of-week shape**: weekends for short trips, midweek for restful stays, Saturday-to-Saturday for full weeks,
   weekend nights for nightlife or time with others.
4. **Timing**: sooner when the guest needs a break, with enough lead time to arrange travel.

**Response (shown to guests):** `recommended` and up to two `alternatives`, each with `label` ("Best overall",
"Best weather", "Another good option"), `check_in`, `check_out`, weekdays, `nights`, `reasons[]` and
`considerations[]`; plus `summary` (1–2 sentences, AI-written and checked, or a factual fallback) and
`availability_note`, which **must be shown with the dates**:

> These dates are a suggestion based on your answers. We haven't checked live availability or prices for them, so we
> can't say they're available or the lowest-priced option.

`basis`, `factor_scores` and `origin` are for the team, not for display. When the guest accepts a suggestion, send
the dates to `/get_suggested_city` as `travel_timing: "exact_dates"` with `check_in_date` / `check_out_date`.

## 8. Viator experiences

With `VIATOR_API_KEY` set, every planned experience is matched to a Viator product near its stop's base (only when
the product title genuinely matches). Each matched item gets:

```json
"viator": {"product_code": "123KAYAK", "title": "Clayoquot Sound Sea Kayak Tour", "booking_url": "https://www.viator.com/…",
           "rating": 4.9, "review_count": 412, "from_price": 129.0, "currency": "USD",
           "runs_on_date": true, "date_price": 102.2},
"activity_cost": 102.2, "price_source": "viator_schedule", "availability_status": "SCHEDULED"
```

- **Exact dates:** the price for that date and whether the experience runs that day come from Viator's
  availability schedule. If it doesn't run that day, one guest note says so.
- **No exact dates:** Viator's "from" price per person (`price_source: "viator_from_price"`).
- Show "From $X per person · ★ rating (reviews) on Viator" with a link to `booking_url` (it carries our partner ID).
- The price breakdown's experiences line says how many experiences are priced from Viator.
- Viator asks partners to run its live availability check only at booking time, so items are `SCHEDULED`, never
  `CONFIRMED`, and `booking_status.ready_to_book` stays `false`.

## 9. Not included yet

Live room rates and a verified "ready to book" status need the Booking.com Demand API. Until then stay prices are
estimates and `booking_status.ready_to_book` stays `false`. Ferry sailing times and seasonal closures are not
checked. Transfers add a ferry buffer and a note to confirm sailings.
