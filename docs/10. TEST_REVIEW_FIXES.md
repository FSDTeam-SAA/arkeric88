# 10. Customer Test Review Fixes

Fixes in the AI service for the issues found in the October 2026 customer tests (five
questionnaire tests, the pricing catalogue request and the LiteAPI questions). Intake mapping and
scoring versions are now `2026-10-30.1`.

## Matching changes

- **Travel effort against trip length.** When the departure point is located, every destination's
  straight-line distance is compared with the trip length: up to 1,500 km suits 2+ nights, 4,000 km
  4+, 8,000 km 6+, beyond that 9+. A shorter trip keeps part of the 10 travel points and takes a
  penalty of up to 20 points, with a trade-off line such as "A long journey for a 5-night trip: about
  15,820 km from Austin…". Nothing is excluded for it. Test 1 now returns Ubud 87, Rishikesh 78,
  Sedona 72 instead of Ubud 100, Rishikesh 92, Baa Atoll 85.
- **Budget fit from price estimates.** Put the workbook's `PRICE_ESTIMATES_1000` sheet in `data/`
  as a CSV whose name ends in `PRICE_ESTIMATES_1000.csv`, with `destination_id` plus low, typical
  and high nightly columns (USD per room, per night, two adults). It is joined by `destination_id`;
  the 21-column catalogue is unchanged. Typical estimate within budget: no change. Only the low
  estimate within budget: −5 and "May stretch your budget". Low estimate above budget: −10 and
  "Likely above your budget". Never excluded; an open-ended budget is never penalised. Cards show
  `verification.price_estimate` (with an estimated stay total for rooms × nights) and an "Estimated
  stays: about $X–$Y… an estimate, not a quote" line. Without the file, budget fit stays `UNKNOWN`.
- **Goal order is fixed.** Without a main goal, goals follow the form's order, so the same answers
  always give the same label and results (Test 4 showed "Reflective" because the order changed).
- **Card text.** "For restoration and reflection: nature, yoga and jungle." replaces "Room to feel
  restored and reflective: creative setting and nature." Moments name what backs them: "spa and
  wellness (yoga)". Catalogue shorthand such as "may not suit guest" now reads "may not suit
  everyone".
- **No suggestion to downgrade.** The no-result message for an accessibility requirement no longer
  suggests "I'd prefer to avoid this".
- **A city the map can't find isn't swapped for another.** "Testville, TX" used to be matched to
  Stephenville, TX and treated as verified. A map result must now match what the guest typed
  (allowing small typos and airport codes); otherwise the origin is `UNVERIFIED`. If the guest named
  a state or region, Nearby stays in that part of the world without claiming a distance.

## LiteAPI

- Authentication with the production key works: a live search returned 28 offers for an Austin
  hotel (7–12 December 2026, 2 adults).
- **Hotel matching fixed.** A supplier name that adds a brand ("The Driskill, in The Unbound
  Collection by Hyatt") now matches when the property is within 250 m.
- Live rates still need exact dates. Searching dates inside a flexible window is not done yet.
- Enable on the live server with `LITEAPI_API_KEY` and `LITEAPI_ENABLED=true`. Tests now always
  run with LiteAPI off.
